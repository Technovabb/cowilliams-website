How we work

We plan, supply and build.

One company with its own machines, its own materials and more than 50 years of work across the region.

  • Our own fleet

    It started with a single tractor. Today our full fleet moves the earth every day, and we're so well equipped that we rent it out too.

  • Our own quarries and lab

    Quarries in Barbados and St. Lucia and a sand plant in Barbados. Products are checked for sieve analysis, proctors, specific gravity, extraction and penetration.

  • An operator with every rental

    Every machine is rented with a trained, professional operator, so the job gets done safely.

  • Regional experience

    Offices in Barbados and St. Lucia, with work in St. Vincent & the Grenadines, Antigua, St. Kitts and the Bahamas.
