We have quarries in Barbados and St. Lucia, and a sand plant in Barbados that produces some of the highest quality compounds on the market.

We uphold quality control and follow international procedures to ensure longevity and durability for each of our many products.

Our sand plant

At our sand plant in Barbados, the sand is washed, cleaned, sieved, prepped and sorted by size. The plant also produces lime and stone: products that are tried, tested, proven and exported for construction around the Caribbean.
