Why C.O. Williams

Work with a Caribbean construction leader

It started with a single tractor. Today our fleet moves the earth every day, on jobs from a home driveway to an airport runway.

  • 50+ years, Barbadian born

    A Barbadian company with more than 50 years of history, built on quality, a complete range of services and integrity.

  • Five divisions

    Civil, marine, asphalt, quarries and equipment rental, all under one roof.

  • Work across the Caribbean

    Offices in Barbados and St. Lucia, with projects in St. Vincent & the Grenadines, Antigua, St. Kitts and the Bahamas.

  • A full fleet

    Excavators, bulldozers, backhoes, rollers, dump trucks and more: a full fleet that moves the earth daily.
