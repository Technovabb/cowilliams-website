Port St. Charles

A luxury marina development on the platinum coast of Barbados.

Port St. Charles is a luxury marina development on the platinum coast, and a topic of great pride for C.O. Williams.

The site brings together exclusive villas, condos and apartments with an inland lagoon area and berthing facilities.

Our other marine work in Barbados includes the boulder containment wall for the Bridgetown Port extension and dolo coastal protection at Bridgetown Fisheries.
