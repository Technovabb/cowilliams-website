Apes Hill Golf Course

Civil works for the Apes Hill Golf Course in Barbados.

Plan a civil project All projects

Apes Hill Golf Course is one of our civil projects in Barbados, alongside the Green Monkey and Molyneaux courses at Sandy Lane and the course at Durants.

On site at Apes Hill

Our photos from the site, shown below, include a newly paved road with kerbing that curves through green hills with the golf course behind, and a second road edged with a band of paving stones, climbing a hill beside a new building.

Next to the building, the ground is graded and staked out, with a roller and a skid steer on site. A C.O. Williams skid steer stands in front of the building.
