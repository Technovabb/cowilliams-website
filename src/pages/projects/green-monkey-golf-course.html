The famous Green Monkey Golf Course is a par-72, 7,343-yard course at Sandy Lane.

This exclusive course has unique features, including lush fairways and exposed rock faces that make it challenging and one of a kind.

Seen from above, the course winds between exposed rock faces, sand bunkers and ponds.
