Our story

Over 50 years of moving the earth

C.O. Williams Construction Ltd. started with a single tractor.

Today, over 50 years after that tractor debuted, we have a full fleet that moves the earth daily. In fact, we're so well equipped that we also do a high level of rentals.

We strongly believe in pleasing customers, and we do this by providing excellent quality, a complete range of services and operating with integrity. Just ask around about our reputation as the market leader in the Caribbean construction industry.

C.O. Williams Construction Limited is a Barbadian born company that also has offices in St. Lucia. We stand proud of our history of working throughout the region, moving the earth to please anywhere we go.
