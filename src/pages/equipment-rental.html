Questions

Renting from C.O. Williams

Quick answers about renting a machine and operator from us. For anything else, call or email the rentals desk.

Do I get an operator with the machine?

Yes. All of our equipment is rented with a professional operator.

How do I get a price?

Contact us for pricing. Request a quote or call the rentals desk on (246) 436-3910. Tell us the machine and the dates, and we'll come back with availability and pricing.

Can I rent equipment for a home renovation?

Yes. We rent for big construction jobs and home renovations alike, and every machine comes with its operator.

Which machines can I rent?

Our fleet includes small and large excavators, dump trucks, bulldozers, skid steers, backhoes, rollers, trucks and compressors. If you're not sure which machine you need, tell us about the job and we'll advise.
