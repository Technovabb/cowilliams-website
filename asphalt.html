Services · Asphalt

Asphalt works

Driveways to runways — paving, resurfacing and slurry seal, with materials we produce ourselves.

Request a quote See our projects

Whether it's the road you take to school or the runway that lands your aircraft, all kinds of people and machines depend on all kinds of surfaces.

That's why we make sure C.O. Williams Asphalt and Quarries Ltd leads the way in the region. We use top-of-the-line equipment to produce materials that are major ingredients in so many construction jobs, from roadways to runways.

What we do

  • Asphalt paving
  • Road construction & rehabilitation
  • Highways
  • Airport runway resurfacing
  • Car parks & container parks
  • Home driveways
  • Slurry seal for driveways and surfaces
  • Asphalt & aggregate production

Major asphalt works

  • Sir Garfield Sobers Gymnasium car park
  • Bridgetown Port container park
  • Highways (Barbados, St. Vincent, St. Lucia, Antigua, Bequia, St. Kitts)
